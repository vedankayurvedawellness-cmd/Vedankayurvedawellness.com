Understanding fatty liver

Fatty liver means extra fat has built up in liver cells. It is very common, and in many people it causes no symptoms at first. It is often found by chance on an ultrasound or when blood tests show raised liver enzymes such as SGPT (ALT) and SGOT (AST).

Common causes

  • Excess body weight, especially around the waist
  • Diabetes or pre-diabetes and insulin resistance
  • High cholesterol or triglycerides
  • Regular alcohol use
  • Fried, sugary and heavily processed food, and a sedentary routine
  • Some medicines, and a family tendency

Symptoms to watch for

  • Tiredness and heaviness after meals
  • Discomfort or fullness in the upper right abdomen
  • Bloating, poor digestion or reduced appetite
  • Often none at all, which is why a check-up matters

How Ayurveda looks at it

Ayurveda links liver fat to weak digestion (agni) and the build-up of ama and excess kapha and meda. Care is therefore built around the person, not just the scan result: diet, daily routine, suitable herbal medicines and, where the doctor advises, Panchkarma therapies.

What a supervised programme at Vedank includes

  1. Consultation and review of your reports: ultrasound, liver function tests, sugar and lipid profile.
  2. A personalised diet and routine: simple changes you can keep up.
  3. Ayurvedic medicines, prescribed by our doctor to suit your condition.
  4. Panchkarma and supportive therapies, if the doctor advises them.
  5. Follow-up and repeat tests, so progress is measured and not guessed.

Daily habits that help

  • Walk or exercise for at least 30 minutes on most days
  • Cut down sweets, sugary drinks, fried food and bakery items
  • Stop alcohol if you have fatty liver
  • Eat at regular times and avoid late heavy dinners
  • Aim for gradual weight loss if you are overweight

When to see a doctor soon

Get medical advice promptly if you have yellow eyes or skin, dark urine, swelling of the legs or abdomen, vomiting blood or black stools, or severe abdominal pain. These need urgent attention.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Ayurveda treat fatty liver?

Many people with fatty liver benefit from a supervised programme of diet, routine and Ayurvedic medicines, along with regular monitoring. Results depend on the grade, the cause and how well the plan is followed. Our doctor will explain what to expect after reviewing your reports.

How long does fatty liver treatment take?

It depends on the grade and your reports. Most programmes are planned over a few months, with a review and repeat tests along the way.

Do I need to stop alcohol?

Yes. Alcohol adds to liver fat and inflammation, so it should be stopped during treatment.
